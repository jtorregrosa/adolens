The sidebar lists your **Projects** and their **Libraries** (Variable Groups). Use the search box to find projects or groups quickly.

## Loading variable groups

- Load a variable group into the **Left** or **Right** pane with the **L** / **R** buttons.
- You can mark libraries as **Favorites** for quick access.
- The sidebar can be expanded or collapsed to free up space.

Selecting a group loads its variables into the chosen pane. You can then compare two groups by loading one on the left and one on the right.
