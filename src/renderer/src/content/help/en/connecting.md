On the login screen, enter your **Azure DevOps organization URL** (e.g. `https://dev.azure.com/your-org`) and a **Personal Access Token (PAT)**.

## Required permissions

Your PAT must have the right permissions:

- **Library (Variable Groups):** Read & write for Variable Groups
- **User Profile:** Read — to identify your user and access organization resources

Click the link below the PAT field on the login screen for step-by-step token creation and required scopes.

You can choose to **remember the token** between sessions (stored in the OS keychain) or keep it only for the current session.
