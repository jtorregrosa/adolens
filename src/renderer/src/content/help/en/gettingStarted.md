ADOLens is an **Azure DevOps Library Inspector Tool**. It lets you view and edit Variable Groups (Library) in a side-by-side diff view.

After connecting with your organization URL and a Personal Access Token, open **Projects** and **Libraries** from the sidebar. Load one or two variable groups into the left and right panes to compare or edit them.

Use the toolbar to:
- Sync scroll between panes
- Swap left and right panes
- Push your changes back to Azure DevOps
